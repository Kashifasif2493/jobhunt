How to Receive International Payments in Pakistan (2026 Guide)

Published August 2026  ·  6 min read

Landing a remote job or freelance client abroad is only half the battle — actually getting paid in Pakistan without losing a big chunk to fees or delays is the other half. In 2026, there are several reliable ways to receive international payments. Here is a practical breakdown of what actually works.

1. Payoneer

Payoneer is one of the most widely used options among Pakistani freelancers because many platforms (Fiverr, Upwork, and various remote employers) support it directly. You get a US, UK, or EU receiving account number, and funds can be withdrawn straight to your local Pakistani bank account.

💡 Withdrawal to a Pakistani bank account usually takes 1-3 business days, and Payoneer's conversion rate is generally better than a standard bank wire.

2. Wise (formerly TransferWise)

Wise is known for showing the real mid-market exchange rate with a transparent, upfront fee — no hidden markup. It works well for one-off client payments or freelance invoices, though its direct support for Pakistan has some limitations compared to countries like India, so check current account options before relying on it as your only method.

3. Direct Bank Wire (SWIFT)

Some employers, especially larger companies, will pay directly via SWIFT wire transfer to your Pakistani bank account. This works but often comes with higher fees from both the sending and receiving bank, and the exchange rate applied by local banks is usually less favorable than Payoneer or Wise.

4. Setting Up Correctly From Day One

  • Use your legal name exactly as it appears on your CNIC when creating accounts
  • Keep your bank account details and IBAN handy before signing up
  • Declare freelance/remote income properly — check current FBR guidance for freelancers, as tax rules do get updated
  • Avoid sharing your Payoneer or Wise login details with anyone, including "middlemen" offering to help you get paid faster

5. Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Relying on only one payment method — if an account gets temporarily flagged for review, having a backup avoids missed income
  • Ignoring exchange rate differences between providers, especially on larger payments
  • Not verifying your account fully before your first big payment, which can cause delays

Final Tip

Set up your payment method before you need it, not after landing your first client or job offer. Verification can take a few days, and you do not want that holding up your first paycheck.
