Kickresume Review 2026: Is It Worth It?

Published August 2026  ·  5 min read

Kickresume is one of the more polished resume-builder platforms out there, with AI-assisted writing and a large template library. But before you pay for a subscription, it's worth knowing exactly what you're getting — and what free tools can already do just as well.

What Kickresume Does Well

  • A large selection of modern, ATS-friendly resume templates
  • Built-in AI writing assistant for bullet points and summaries
  • Cover letter builder that matches your resume's design
  • Website/portfolio builder included on higher plans

Where It Falls Short

The free plan is quite limited — most templates and the PDF export are locked behind a paid subscription. For someone applying to dozens of jobs on a tight budget, that cost adds up, especially when several free tools now offer similar core features.

Pricing (as of 2026)

Kickresume runs on a subscription model with monthly and annual plans. The annual plan works out cheaper per month, but if you only need a resume for a short job search, the monthly cost may not be worth it compared to a one-time free option.

Free Alternatives Worth Trying First

ToolBest ForFree Export
Google Docs templatesSimple, fast, fully freeYes
CanvaVisual, creative resumesYes (with watermark limits on some)
Novoresume (free tier)Clean, ATS-friendly layoutsLimited
KickresumeAI writing help, premium designPaid

Our Take

If you want AI-generated bullet points and a wide template library and don't mind paying, Kickresume is a solid, well-built option. But if you're on a budget or just need a clean, professional resume fast, a free tool like Google Docs or Canva combined with the writing tips in our own resume guide will get you 90% of the way there without spending anything.
